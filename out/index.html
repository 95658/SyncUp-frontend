Access Architecture

How SyncUp Works: The 3-Tier Access Model

Engineered as a private B2B platform with zero unauthorized public signups.

01

Organization Provisioning

Platform Owner (Ahmed Abdelfatah) provisions a dedicated, isolated organization environment and assigns the company executive as Organization Admin.

02

Secure Invite & Password Token

Organization Admin receives a cryptographic email invitation, securely sets their credential credentials, and enters their tenant.

03

Teams & Member Orchestration

Org Admin builds internal departments, software projects, and invites engineers & team members to collaborate in real-time.

Strict Multi-Tenancy

Zero cross-tenant contamination. Every database query enforces company-scoped isolation.

Zero Open Signups

No public signup vulnerability. Access is granted solely via authorized invitations.

Low-Latency Socket.IO

Real-time WebSocket protocol handles team chats, card dragging, and voting under 30ms.
